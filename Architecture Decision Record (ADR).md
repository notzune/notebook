in [[Technical writing|technical writing]] and [[Software engineering|software engineering]], ADR stands for architecture decision record. it is a short, structured, text-based document that captures a significant architectural decision, including the context, the rationale, and the consequences of that decision. ADRs are meant to explain ***why*** a decision was made, documenting the thought process at a specific point in time. they prevent teams from forgetting why a particular technology or structure was chosen, stopping the need to re-debate the issues later. they are designed for agile, long-lived projects and stored within the code repository (usually in [[Markdown|markdown]]) and evolve with the project.
