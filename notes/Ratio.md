in [[Mathematics (index)|mathematics]], a ratio shows how many times one [[Real numbers|number]] contains another.

for example if there are eight oranges and sex lemons in a bowl of fruit, then the ratio of oranges to lemons is eight to six (that is $8:6$, which is equivalent to the ratio $4:3$)

similarly the ratio of lemons to oranges is $6:8$ (or $3:4$) and the ratio of oranges to the total amount of fruit is $8:14$ (or $4:7$)
