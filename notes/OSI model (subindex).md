the open systems interconnection model (OSI model) is a conceptual model from the international organization for standardization (ISO).

|Layer|Name|Function|Example Protocols|
|---|---|---|---|
|7|Application|User-facing network services|HTTP, FTP, SMTP, DNS|
|6|Presentation|Encryption, compression, formatting|TLS, SSL|
|5|Session|Manages connections between hosts|NetBIOS|
|4|Transport|End-to-end communication|TCP, UDP|
|3|Network|Routing and logical addressing|IP, ICMP|
|2|Data Link|MAC addressing, frames|Ethernet|
|1|Physical|Electrical signals|Fiber, Ethernet cable
