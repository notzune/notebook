in [[Physics|physics]], refraction is the redirection of a [[Wave|wave]] as it passes from one [[Transmission medium|medium]] to another.

the redirection can be caused by the wave's change in speed or by a change in the medium. refraction of light is the most commonly observed phenomenon, but other waves such as [[Sound wave|sound waves]] and water waves also experience refraction.

how much a wave is refracted is determined by the change in wave speed and the initial direction of wave propagation relative to the direction of change in speed.
