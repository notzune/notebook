an imaginary number is the product of a [[Real numbers|real number]] and the [[Imaginary unit|imaginary unit]] $i$, which is defined by its propery $i^2=-1$.

the [[Square|square]] of an imaginary number $bi$ is $-b^2$, for example $5i$ is an imaginary number and its square is $-25$.

the number [[Zero|zero]] is both real and imaginary.
