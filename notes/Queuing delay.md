in [[Telecommunications|telecommunications]] and [[Computer engineering|computer engineering]], the queuing delay is the time a job waits in a [[Queue|queue]] until it can be executed. it is a key component of [[Network delay|network delay]]. 

in a [[Packet switching|switched]] network, queuing delay is the sum of the delays encountered by a packet between the time of insertion into the network and the time of delivery to the address.

in a data network, the delay is the sum of the delays between the request for service and the establishment of a circuit to the called data terminal equipment. 

if packets arrive faster than the [[Router|router]] can process them (such as in a [[Burst transmission|burst transmission]]) the router puts them into the queue (also called the [[Data buffer|buffer]]) until it can get around to transmitting them.

the length of the queuing delay depends on the number of earlier arriving packets that are already being queued and waiting for the transmission on the link. delay varies from packet to packet so the sum of the delays are averaged to measure and evaluate the queueing delay. 

the number of packets that an ***arriving*** packet might expect to find at the queue is a function of the intensity and nature of the link arriving at the queue. 

the nature of the link could mean things such as the [[Transmission medium|transmission medium]] (such as whether or not it is a [[Ethernet over twisted pair|wired connections]], or the [[Bandwidth|bandwidth]] of the connection)

queuing delays cause [[Packet loss|packet loss]].
