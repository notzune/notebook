in [[Mathematical physics|mathematical physics]], scalar potential describes the situation where the difference in the [[Potential energy|potential energies]] of an object in two different positions depends only on the positions, not upon the path taken by the object in traveling from one position to the other, it is a [[Scalar field|scalar field]] in [[Three-dimensional space|three-space]]: a directionless value ([[Scalar|scalar]]) that depends only on its location. an example is potential energy due to gravity.
