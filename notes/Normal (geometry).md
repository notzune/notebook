in [[Geometry|geometry]], a normal is an [[Mathematical object|object]] (e.g. a [[Line|line]], [[Ray|ray]], or [[Vectors|vector]]) that is [[Perpendicularity|perpendicular]] to a given object.

for example, the normal line to a [[Plane curve|plane curve]] at a given [[Point|point]] is the infinite straight line perpendicular to the [[Tangent|tangent line]] to the curve at the point.
