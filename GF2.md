also denoted as $\mathbb{F}_2$, $Z/2Z$, or $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, is the [[Finite field|finite field]] with two [[Element|elements]] and is also the field with the smallest possible number of elements and is unique if the [[Additive identity|additive identity]] and [[Multiplicative identity|multiplicative identity]] are denoted by $0$ and $1$ respectively.

the elements of GF(2) may be identified with the two possible values of a [[Bit|bit]] and to the [[Boolean|boolean values]] true and false.

its definition is defined as the usual addition of [[Integer|integers]] but [[modulo]] 2 and corresponds to the table below:

| **+** | **0** | **1** |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **0** |   0   |   1   |
| **1** |   1   |   0   |

if the elements of GF(2) are seen as boolean values, then the addition is the same as that of the [[Logic gates|logical]] [[XOR]] operation. since each [[Element|element]] equals its [[Additive Inverse|opposite]], subtraction is the same operation as addition.
